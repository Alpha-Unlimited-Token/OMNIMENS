## 1. PURPOSE

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship involving the OMNIMENS(TM) neuromorphic AI platform, autonomous consciousness architecture, and related technologies ("Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose certain Confidential Information to the other Party.

## 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

**2.1** "Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information disclosed by either Party to the other, whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by any other means, including but not limited to:

**(a) Technical Information:**
- Source code, object code, algorithms, and software architecture
- Neural network designs, consciousness models, and wiring topologies
- Phi computation methods, OAI formulas, and scoring methodologies
- Agent genesis, evolution, and self-modification frameworks
- Prompt engineering techniques and AI training methodologies
- Database schemas, API designs, and system integration patterns
- Hardware abstraction layers and robotic embodiment designs
- All Gen 1 and Gen 2 OMNIMENS engine architectures

**(b) Business Information:**
- Business plans, strategies, and financial information
- Customer and user data, usage analytics, and growth metrics
- Pricing models, revenue projections, and cost structures
- Marketing strategies and competitive analyses
- Partnership and licensing arrangements
- Patent applications (published and unpublished)

**(c) Other Information:**
- Any information marked "Confidential," "Proprietary," or similar designation
- Any information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure
- All notes, analyses, compilations, studies, or other materials prepared by the Receiving Party that contain or reflect Confidential Information

**2.2** Confidential Information does NOT include information that:

(a) Was publicly available at the time of disclosure through no fault of the Receiving Party;
(b) Becomes publicly available after disclosure through no fault of the Receiving Party;
(c) Was rightfully in the Receiving Party's possession before disclosure, without restriction;
(d) Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Confidential Information; or
(e) Is rightfully received from a third party without restriction and without breach of any obligation of confidentiality.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

**3.1** The Receiving Party shall:

(a) Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence;
(b) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party;
(c) Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
(d) Limit access to Confidential Information to those employees, contractors, and advisors ("Representatives") who (i) have a need to know, and (ii) are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement;
(e) Protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care used to protect its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care;
(f) Promptly notify the Disclosing Party of any unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information.

**3.2** The Receiving Party shall NOT:

(a) Reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any software or technology constituting Confidential Information;
(b) Copy, reproduce, or create derivative works from Confidential Information except as necessary for the Purpose;
(c) Use Confidential Information to develop competing products or services;
(d) Remove or alter any proprietary notices, trade secret legends, or copyright markings;
(e) Store Confidential Information on unsecured systems or transmit via unencrypted channels.

## 4. SPECIFIC PROTECTIONS FOR OMNIMENS TECHNOLOGY

**4.1** The Parties acknowledge that the OMNIMENS neuromorphic architecture, consciousness models, agent frameworks, and self-evolution systems constitute highly valuable trade secrets of the Company.

**4.2** The Receiving Party specifically agrees:

(a) Not to attempt to reproduce, replicate, or create any system substantially similar to the OMNIMENS architecture;
(b) Not to use any knowledge of the OMNIMENS wiring topology, agent genesis system, or consciousness field implementations for any competing product;
(c) Not to train any AI model or system using OMNIMENS source code, prompts, or architectural patterns;
(d) Not to disclose the existence or nature of OMNIMENS proprietary algorithms, including but not limited to the OAI formula, Phi computation methods, or neural mesh architecture, to any third party;
(e) That any code, documentation, or materials provided for evaluation purposes remain the exclusive property of the Company.

## 5. TERM AND TERMINATION

**5.1** This Agreement is effective from the Effective Date and continues for _______ years ("Term"), unless terminated earlier by either Party with thirty (30) days written notice.

**5.2** The confidentiality obligations under this Agreement shall survive termination and continue for a period of five (5) years after the Term, or for as long as the information qualifies as a trade secret under applicable law, whichever is longer.

**5.3** Upon termination or upon request, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and all copies thereof, and certify such return or destruction in writing.

## 6. OWNERSHIP

**6.1** All Confidential Information remains the exclusive property of the Disclosing Party. No license, right, or interest in any Confidential Information is granted except the limited right to use such information for the Purpose.

**6.2** Nothing in this Agreement grants any rights under any patent, copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property right of either Party.

## 7. REMEDIES

**7.1** The Parties acknowledge that any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. Accordingly, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive and other equitable relief, in addition to all other remedies available at law or in equity, without the necessity of posting a bond.

**7.2** The prevailing Party in any action to enforce this Agreement shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorney's fees and costs.

## 8. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

**8.1** If the Receiving Party is compelled by law, regulation, or legal process to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall:

(a) Provide prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party (to the extent legally permitted);
(b) Cooperate with the Disclosing Party in seeking a protective order or other appropriate remedy;
(c) Disclose only the minimum amount of Confidential Information required; and
(d) Use reasonable efforts to ensure confidential treatment of any disclosed information.
