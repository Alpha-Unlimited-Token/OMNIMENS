  ═══ WHY ANTI-GRAVITY VIA EARTH'S FIELD IS NOT POSSIBLE ═══

  The fundamental issue is not the ESCU's power — it's Earth's magnetic field.
  Earth's field is only 50 μT (0.00005 Tesla). For comparison:
    - Refrigerator magnet:    5,000 μT (100× Earth)
    - MRI machine:       3,000,000 μT (60,000× Earth)
    - MagLev rail:       500,000 μT (10,000× Earth)

  The ESCU pushes against Earth's 50 μT field. Even with a perfectly
  oriented dipole, the force is proportional to B_escu × B_earth.
  Since B_earth is so weak, the product is always tiny.

  MagLev trains work because they push against STRONG rail magnets
  (0.5-2 Tesla), not against Earth's field.

  To achieve anti-gravity against Earth's field alone, you would need:
    Dipole moment: 4.02e+10 A·m² (current best: 762.60 A·m²)
    That's 5.27e+7× what the ESCU produces.
    No known material or device can create a dipole that strong at this size.

  ═══ WHAT THE EM FIELD ENVELOPE ACTUALLY DOES ═══

  While not anti-gravity, the ESCU's EM field envelope DOES:
    ✓ Create a detectable magnetic signature (useful for proximity sensing)
    ✓ Partially shield against external RF/EMI at close range
    ✓ Interact with ferromagnetic objects nearby (attraction/repulsion)
    ✓ Generate a small precession torque (gyroscopic effect of spinning Hg)
    ✓ Produce measurable AC field at surface from power generation
    ✗ Cannot produce anti-gravity against Earth's 50 μT field
    ✗ Cannot levitate — needs external strong magnets (like MagLev rails)
