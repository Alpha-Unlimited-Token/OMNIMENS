─── SELF-SUSTAINING: THE ENERGY LOOP ───

  For self-sustaining operation, the feedback loop must satisfy:
  Generated_Power > Body_Load + Internal_Losses + Feedback_Losses

  The feedback loop works like this:
  1. Mercury spins → generates electricity (brakes mercury)
  2. Part of electricity → AMFOS coils → accelerates mercury
  3. Rest of electricity → powers the body

  The problem: Step 1 REMOVES kinetic energy from mercury.
  Step 2 puts SOME of it back, but with losses at each conversion:
    - Generator extraction: ~50% efficiency at max power point
    - AMFOS coupling: ~35% efficiency (air gap to liquid metal)
    - Round-trip: 0.50 × 0.35 = 17.5% of extracted power returns
  So for every 100W extracted, only 17.5W returns as rotation.
  The mercury ALWAYS decelerates — it's losing 82.5% per cycle.

  This is not a design flaw — it's conservation of energy.
  No electromagnetic generator can output more power than the
  mechanical energy input driving it. The ESCU's driving energy
  is the mercury's kinetic energy, which is finite.

  Kickstart stores 25.9062 J of kinetic energy in mercury.
  At 2938W load, that's 0.0088 seconds of power.
  Even with zero losses, the stored energy is far too small.

✓ CORRECT DESIGN CHOICES:
  - Mercury as working fluid (best liquid conductor available)
  - Halbach arrays (maximizes field in active region)
  - Opposing rotation layers (doubles relative velocity)
  - Tungsten/Mo/Ta conductors (mercury-resistant, high melting point)
  - Magnetic levitation between layers (zero contact friction)
  - Titanium body frame (non-ferromagnetic, won't interfere with ESCU)
  - Mu-metal shielding (protects server from ESCU field)
  - Saltwater cooling (effective for this thermal load)

✗ ISSUES TO ADDRESS:

  ISSUE 1: VOLTAGE — Homopolar generators make LOW voltage, HIGH current.
    At achievable RPMs, EMF is millivolts to single-digit volts.
    Blueprint specifies 48V output. This requires either:
      a) Disc diameter of 300-500mm (not 96mm)
      b) RPM of 63726 (mercury would experience extreme centrifugal force)
      c) A boost converter to step up the voltage (adds 5-10% loss)

  ISSUE 2: SELF-SUSTAINING LOOP — Energy conservation prevents it.
    Every watt of electricity comes from mercury's kinetic energy.
    Feeding power back to AMFOS returns only ~17.5% (conversion losses).
    Mercury MUST slow down — there's no external energy source to replace
    the energy being extracted.

  ISSUE 3: KINETIC ENERGY STORAGE — Too small at this scale.
    Mercury KE at peak: 25.9062 J
    Body needs: 2938 J every second
    That's 0.0088 seconds of runtime from stored KE alone.

  ISSUE 4: ANTI-GRAVITY — Not achievable with permanent magnets + coils.
    EM force from AMFOS is millinewtons. Body weighs 441 N.
    Need superconducting magnets or entirely different physics.

  FIX 1: SCALE UP THE DISCS
    Increase disc diameter to 300-500mm.
    EMF scales with R². Tripling radius = 9× the voltage.
    Unit would be ~400mm × 300mm (~size of a car battery).
    Still fits in a humanoid torso.

  FIX 2: ADD AN EXTERNAL ENERGY INPUT
    The ESCU concept works as a GENERATOR, not a perpetual motion device.
    It needs a continuous energy INPUT. Options:
    a) Heat differential — thermoelectric elements between hot mercury
       and cool exterior could drive a continuous temperature gradient.
       The body's waste heat from electronics IS a real energy source.
    b) Ambient RF harvesting — adds 1-10W (supplemental only).
    c) Solar panels on body exterior — 100-300W (most practical).
    d) Kinetic energy recovery from walking — 50-200W (proven tech).

  FIX 3: USE ESCU AS HIGH-EFFICIENCY FLYWHEEL + GENERATOR
    Instead of self-sustaining, use as energy storage + conversion:
    - Charge from wall power (48V/20A for 10 minutes = 576 kJ)
    - Mercury stores kinetic energy with VERY low friction
    - Draw power as needed (high-current bursts for actuators)
    - Recharge when near a power source
    This is how real regenerative systems work (EVs, flywheels).

  FIX 4: REDUCE BODY POWER REQUIREMENTS
    Current: 2938W at 115%
    - Use Jetson Orin NX (15W) instead of AGX (60W) for general tasks
    - Spin up AGX only for heavy AI workloads
    - Use efficient actuators with regenerative braking
    - Sleep unused sensors
    - Target: average draw of 600-800W

  FIX 5: SERIES-STACK MULTIPLE ESCU UNITS
    3 ESCU units with outputs in series = 3× the voltage.
    Each unit is 120mm × 180mm = still fits in a humanoid chest.
