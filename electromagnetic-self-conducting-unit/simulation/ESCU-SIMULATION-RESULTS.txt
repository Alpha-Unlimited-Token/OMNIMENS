═══ MAGNETIC CONFINEMENT PRESSURE ═══

  The mercury in each gap is squeezed from ALL directions:

  TOP:     Layer above, same polarity face → REPELS downward
           B = 0.85T → Pressure = 287.5 kPa = 2.84 atm

  BOTTOM:  Layer below, same polarity face → REPELS upward
           B = 0.85T → Pressure = 287.5 kPa = 2.84 atm

  RIGHT (outer): Shell magnets → push INWARD
           B at disc edge = 0.6T → Pressure = 143.2 kPa = 1.41 atm

  LEFT (inner): Lower B near shaft → pressure gradient pushes inward
           Creates radial confinement gradient

  TOTAL CONFINEMENT: Mercury under ~7.1 atm magnetic pressure
  Mercury is TRAPPED and COMPRESSED by magnetic fields on all sides.

  DYNAMIC EFFECT — MAGNETIC PERISTALTIC PUMP:
  As layers counter-rotate with different magnet counts,
  the repulsion pressure PEAKS and VALLEYS move around the ring.
  Different counts mean pressure peaks from top and bottom DON'T align.
  This creates a TRAVELLING PRESSURE WAVE — like a peristaltic pump.
  The mercury flows from high-pressure to low-pressure zones,
  creating rotational flow. The shell pushing from the sides ADDS
  to this by creating a shear force at the rim.
