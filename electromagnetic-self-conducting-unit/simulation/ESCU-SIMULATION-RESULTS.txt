═══ EMBEDDED CONDUCTOR MECHANISM ═══

  How the tungsten strips embedded in each disc work:

  1. INDUCTION: As Layer 1 (CW) passes Layer 2 (CCW),
     each tungsten strip on Layer 1 sweeps through Layer 2's B field.
     EMF is induced in each strip: EMF = B × v_relative × strip_length

  2. CURRENT FLOW: The induced EMF drives current THROUGH the mercury
     gap between layers. Mercury completes the electrical circuit.
     Path: Strip(L1) → Mercury(gap) → Strip(L2) → return via mercury

  3. LORENTZ FORCE: That current flowing through mercury is moving
     charge in a magnetic field. F = I × L × B pushes the mercury.

  4. PATTERN DESIGN: The intricate strip layout is angled/curved so
     the current paths produce Lorentz force IN the rotation direction.
     This is the same principle as an electric motor's windings —
     the pattern converts electrical energy back into rotational force.

  5. SELF-REINFORCING LOOP:
     Faster spin → more EMF → more current → more Lorentz force → ...
     QUESTION: Does this loop gain energy or just recirculate?

║  EMBEDDED CONDUCTOR COUPLING — HONEST PHYSICS                       ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  The conductor coupling is REAL and does useful work:
  At peak: 314.85W of Lorentz force on mercury.

  But here's the honest energy accounting:
  The CURRENT driving the Lorentz force is powered by the MOTION.
  It's a regenerative loop:
    Mercury spins → strips cut B field → EMF → current in mercury
    → Lorentz force on mercury → maintains spin

  This loop has LOSSES at each step:
    - Ohmic loss in tungsten strips (I²R)
    - Ohmic loss in mercury path (I²R)
    - The Lorentz force recovery is always LESS than the Lenz braking

  What the conductor pattern DOES accomplish:
    1. REDUCES braking — recovers ~40% of Lenz's law braking force
    2. EXTENDS coast time — mercury spins longer after kickstart
    3. BOOSTS effective B field — induced B adds to permanent B
    4. IMPROVES efficiency — less energy wasted as heat

  What it CANNOT do:
    Create energy from nothing. The conductor pattern is ingenious
    engineering, but it's recovering energy that was already in the
    system — not adding new energy from outside.
