║  37° ANGLED MAGNET CONCEPT ANALYSIS                                 ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  CONCEPT:
  Each magnet segment has its leading and trailing faces cut at 37°,
  with opposing angles on opposite ends. As counter-rotating layers
  pass each other:

  Standard flat magnets:
    ┌────┐   ┌────┐     Symmetric forces.
    │ N  │   │ S  │     Attract then repel equally.
    └────┘   └────┘     Net tangential force = 0.

  37° angled magnets:
    ┌────╱   ╲────┐     Asymmetric gap distance.
    │ N ╱     ╲ S │     Attract at SMALLER gap (stronger).
    └──╱       ╲──┘     Repel at LARGER gap (weaker).
                        Net tangential force > 0 → PUSH.

║  37° ANGLE ANALYSIS — WHAT IT ACTUALLY DOES                         ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  The angled magnet concept is sound in principle.
  It DOES create a net tangential force — the asymmetric gap
  geometry means attraction is stronger than repulsion.

  BUT — the energy has to come from somewhere. There are only
  two possible sources:

  SOURCE 1: THE MAGNETIC FIELD ITSELF
    Permanent magnets store energy: E = B²V/(2μ₀)
    Total energy in disc magnets: 64.74 J (0.0180 Wh)
    At 2938W body load, that's 0.022 seconds of power.
    This means the magnets would demagnetize rapidly if this
    were the only energy source. N52 magnets do slowly lose
    strength when doing work — this accelerates that process.

  SOURCE 2: COGGING TORQUE REDISTRIBUTION
    In a symmetric magnet layout, the attract and repel forces
    cancel perfectly over one full revolution = zero net work.
    The 37° angle breaks symmetry so there IS net tangential force.
    But this force comes from the MAGNETIC POTENTIAL ENERGY of the
    configuration. Once the magnets reach their lowest-energy
    arrangement, they lock there (magnetic detent) and stop.

    Think of it like a ball rolling down a hill with bumps:
    the angled faces create the bumps, but the ball still needs
    an external push to get over each one.

  WHAT THE ANGLE ACTUALLY HELPS WITH:
    1. REDUCED COGGING — Smoother rotation (less 'jerky' motion)
    2. TIMING OPTIMIZATION — Can tune when the attractive/repulsive
       pulses occur relative to the conductor position
    3. SLIGHTLY BETTER EMF WAVEFORM — More sinusoidal, less pulsed
    4. SMALL NET IMPULSE — Real but tiny compared to body load

╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  HOW TO MAKE THE ANGLE CONCEPT MORE EFFECTIVE                       ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  The angle idea has merit but needs amplification:

  OPTION A: VARIABLE RELUCTANCE DESIGN
    Instead of angling the magnet faces, use shaped pole pieces
    (soft iron teeth between magnets) that create variable
    reluctance as layers rotate. This is how switched reluctance
    motors work — proven technology, very efficient.
    Combined with the AMFOS coils (electronically timed),
    this could significantly improve conversion efficiency.

  OPTION B: VERNIER EFFECT
    Use DIFFERENT numbers of magnets on adjacent layers.
    Example: Layer 1 has 16 segments, Layer 2 has 18 segments.
    This creates a 'vernier' effect where the magnetic coupling
    constantly shifts, creating a continuous tangential force
    component rather than discrete pulses. This is how vernier
    permanent magnet machines work (10-20% better than standard).

  OPTION C: COMBINE ANGLES + VERNIER + LARGER DISCS
    - 37° angled faces (your idea) for force asymmetry
    - Vernier segment count (16 vs 18) for continuous coupling
    - Increase disc to 300mm diameter for 9× the EMF
    - Add regenerative braking from actuators (real energy input)
    This combination could produce a genuinely useful power system.
