## 1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW

The ESCU Spherical Model is a self-sustaining electromagnetic power core consisting
of 6 concentric spherical shells of curved octagonal N52 NdFeB magnets, with mercury
(Hg) filling all gaps between shells. After a one-time 30-second electrical kickstart,
the mercury rotates indefinitely, generating continuous electrical current through
electromagnetic induction. The magnetic field is permanent and does not deplete.

### Edge Angle: 37 Degrees
Every octagon edge is cut at a 37-degree angle. This creates:
- Interlocking pressure between neighboring magnets
- Tangential magnetic shear that drives mercury rotation
- Better mechanical stability of the tessellation on the curved shell

### Curvature
Every magnet is curved to match the radius of its shell. Outer shell magnets
have gentle curvature (large radius). Inner shell magnets have tighter curvature
(small radius). This is critical — flat magnets would leave gaps on a sphere.

## 13. ASSEMBLY PROCEDURE

### Required Tools
- Clean room (Class 10,000 minimum — mercury handling)
- Spherical jig set (one per shell radius)
- Vacuum fill station (for mercury)
- Magnetization fixture (for final magnetization of assembled shells)
- Copper tubing bender (8mm radius-specific dies)
- Torque wrench (M6 Ti bolts)
- Mercury-rated PPE (respirator, gloves, face shield)

### Assembly Steps (Inside Out)

**Step 1: Core Bearing**
- Place ceramic Si₃N₄ core bearing (15mm radius, polished) in center jig
- Verify roundness < 0.01mm tolerance

**Step 2: Shell 6 Assembly**
- Arrange 8 curved octagonal magnets on R=22mm spherical jig
- Orient all magnets S_out (south pole facing outward)
- Verify 37-degree edge angles mate correctly
- Bond magnets with high-temp adhesive (rated 200°C)
- Verify Flower of Life conductor pattern on both faces, through-holes clear

**Step 3: Shell 6 → Core Mating**
- Place Shell 6 assembly over core bearing
- Verify 7mm gap between core surface and shell inner face
- Mercury will fill this gap later

**Step 4: Shell 5 Assembly**
- Arrange 18 curved octagonal magnets on R=34mm spherical jig
- Orient all magnets N_out (north pole facing outward)
- Bond with high-temp adhesive
- Verify conductor pattern and through-holes

**Step 5: Shell 5 Mating**
- Place Shell 5 over Shell 6 assembly
- Verify 5mm gap (Shell 5 inner at 27mm, Shell 6 outer at 22mm)
- Use alignment pins to maintain concentricity

**Step 6: Shell 4 Assembly**
- 34 curved octagonal magnets on R=46mm jig
- Orient S_out
- Bond, verify conductors

**Step 7: Shell 4 Mating**
- Place over Shell 5, verify 5mm gap

**Step 8: Shell 3 Assembly**
- 54 curved octagonal magnets on R=58mm jig
- Orient N_out
- Bond, verify conductors

**Step 9: Shell 3 Mating**
- Place over Shell 4, verify 5mm gap

**Step 10: Shell 2 Assembly**
- 80 curved octagonal magnets on R=70mm jig
- Orient S_out
- Bond, verify conductors

**Step 11: Shell 2 Mating**
- Place over Shell 3, verify 5mm gap

**Step 12: Shell 1 Assembly**
- 112 curved octagonal magnets on R=82mm jig
- Orient N_out
- Bond, verify conductors

**Step 13: Shell 1 Mating**
- Place over Shell 2, verify 5mm gap

**Step 14: Mercury Fill**
- Place assembled shell stack in vacuum chamber
- Draw vacuum to remove all air from gaps
- Inject 1,093 cm³ of 99.99% pure mercury through fill port
- Mercury fills ALL gaps under vacuum (no air bubbles)
- Seal fill port with tungsten plug, weld shut

**Step 15: Outer Casing**
- Lower hemisphere of outer casing (N_out, 5mm thick N52)
- Place mercury-filled shell assembly into lower hemisphere
- Verify 3mm gap between casing inner surface and Shell 1 outer surface
- Top off mercury through upper port
- Seal upper hemisphere of outer casing
- Weld casing halves (electron beam weld, hermetic seal)

**Step 16: Charging Node Installation**
- Install 4 tungsten charging nodes through pre-drilled casing holes
- 2 positive, 2 negative, at 90-degree spacing
- Seal each node with silicone O-ring
- Verify electrical continuity to mercury

**Step 17: Final Verification**
- X-ray inspection: verify no air bubbles in mercury gaps
- Dimensional check: 180mm OD +/- 0.5mm
- Shake test: no audible movement (mercury fills all space)
- Resistance check: <0.1 ohm between any two charging nodes
- Weight check: 15.8 kg +/- 0.3 kg

**Step 18: Copper Wrap + Coolant**
- Form copper tubing (8mm OD) into 14-loop spiral on sphere jig
- Place ESCU sphere into copper wrap cradle
- Secure with 4 Ti brackets to endoskeleton mounting frame
- Connect feed/return lines to saltwater reservoir
- Fill coolant system: 730mL saltwater (3.5% NaCl + 200ppm Na₂MoO₄)
- Pressure test: 3 bar for 30 minutes, no leaks

**Step 19: Kickstart**
- Connect external power supply to 4 tungsten charging nodes
- Apply 960W (480W per positive node pair) for 30 seconds
- Total energy: 29kJ delivered, ~10kJ coupled to mercury
- Mercury begins rotating within 2 seconds
- Reaches steady-state RPM within 5 seconds
- Remove external power supply
- Mercury continues spinning indefinitely under its own power
- Verify electrical output at charging nodes: >10V EMF
