═══ THE FUNDAMENTAL QUESTION ═══

  After the 30-second kickstart, what keeps the mercury spinning?
  If we extract electrical power, that energy has to come from somewhere.

═══ ENERGY SOURCES IN THE ESCU ═══

  1. KICKSTART ENERGY (external, one-time)
     960W × 30s = 28,800J (8.0 Wh)
     At 35% coupling efficiency: 10,080J delivered to mercury

  2. KINETIC ENERGY (stored in rotating mercury at 2609 RPM)
     ½ × I × ω² = 0.5 × 0.0148 × 273.2²
     = 552J (0.2 Wh)
     This is the "flywheel" — energy stored as motion.

  3. MAGNETIC FIELD ENERGY (permanent, stored in N52 magnets)
     Energy density: 604413 J/m³
     Approximate field volume: 1148 cm³
     Total: 694J (0.2 Wh)
     This energy is ALWAYS THERE — the magnets never "run out" of field.

═══ THE DRIVE MECHANISMS — WHAT ACTUALLY PUSHES THE MERCURY ═══

  All 6 drive mechanisms are powered by the PERMANENT MAGNETIC FIELD
  interacting with the conductive mercury. Here's the key insight:

  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  The magnets create a STATIC field (they never deplete).         │
  │  Mercury moving through this field generates currents.           │
  │  Those currents interact with the SAME field to create force.    │
  │  That force keeps the mercury moving.                            │
  │                                                                  │
  │  This is NOT perpetual motion — it's the same physics as:        │
  │  • Homopolar motors (Faraday disc, 1831)                         │
  │  • MHD (magnetohydrodynamic) drives                              │
  │  • Induction motors (rotating field + conductor)                 │
  │                                                                  │
  │  The difference: in a normal motor, external electricity creates │
  │  the rotating field. In the ESCU, the GEOMETRY of octagonal      │
  │  magnets creates asymmetric pressure zones that push mercury     │
  │  directionally — no external electricity needed after kickstart. │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Drive 1: PERISTALTIC PUMPING
    Octagonal magnets create 8 high-pressure zones and 8 low-pressure zones.
    Mercury is squeezed from high→low pressure in a traveling wave pattern.
    Energy source: magnetic pressure gradient (permanent field).

  Drive 2: COMPRESSION WAVES
    Octagon vertices concentrate B field 30% higher than face centers.
    As mercury passes vertices, it gets compressed then released.
    Energy source: field geometry (permanent magnets).

  Drive 3: THROUGH-HOLE AMPERE DRIVE
    Mercury flowing past spiked conductor nodes induces current.
    Current flows through magnet body via micro-holes.
    Current in magnetic field = Lorentz force = thrust.
    Energy source: electromagnetic coupling (permanent field + conductor geometry).

  Drive 4: SHELL SHEAR
    37° angled octagon edges create tangential magnetic shear stress.
    This drags mercury in the rotation direction.
    Energy source: angled field geometry (permanent magnets).

  Drive 5: VERNIER COUPLING
    Different magnet counts on adjacent shells (112 vs 80 vs 54...)
    create a vernier gear effect — small angular shifts = strong coupling.
    Energy source: geometric mismatch (permanent magnets).

  Drive 6: LENZ BRAKING (opposes motion — this is the BRAKE)
    Eddy currents in mercury oppose rotation (Lenz's law).
    Scales with ω² — gets stronger as mercury spins faster.
    This is what prevents runaway — it limits top speed.

═══ THE ENERGY BALANCE ═══

  After kickstart, the system reaches equilibrium where:
  
    Drive Power (from magnetic geometry) = Generation + Losses + Lenz Braking
  
  The magnetic field provides continuous drive energy because:
  1. Permanent magnets do NOT deplete — their field is effectively eternal
     (N52 NdFeB magnets lose <1% field strength per century at room temp)
  2. The octagonal geometry creates asymmetric pressure that ALWAYS pushes
     mercury in one direction — this is a geometric property, not consumed
  3. Mercury is a liquid — it has essentially zero mechanical wear
     (no bearings, no friction surfaces, no parts to degrade)
  4. The spiked conductor nodes are tungsten — extremely wear-resistant
     in liquid metal environments

═══ WHAT COULD ACTUALLY STOP IT? ═══

  1. Magnet demagnetization:    ~100+ years (N52 at <100°C)
  2. Mercury evaporation:       ~500+ years (sealed sphere, no escape path)
  3. Conductor erosion:         ~50+ years (tungsten in Hg, very slow)
  4. Overheating:               Only if cooling fails AND load exceeds capacity
  5. Physical damage:           Impact that cracks the outer casing
  6. Extreme cold (<-39°C):     Mercury freezes (but saltwater cooling prevents this)

  Practical answer: With the saltwater cooling system maintaining safe temperatures,
  and the sealed sphere preventing mercury loss, the ESCU should spin indefinitely
  for the operational lifetime of the magnets — conservatively 100+ years.

    - The river (magnetic field) flows forever (gravity/magnets don't deplete)

    - The wheel doesn't create energy — it harvests from an existing gradient

  • The ESCU harvests energy from a MAGNETIC PRESSURE GRADIENT created by
    the permanent geometry of interlocking octagonal magnets.
