## 1. NERVOUS SYSTEM — The Body's Control Network

### Central Nervous System (CNS)
- **Brain:** ~86 billion neurons, ~100 trillion synapses
- **Spinal Cord:** 31 pairs of spinal nerves, reflex arc processing
- **Processing Speed:** Neurons fire at 1-100Hz, signals travel 1-120 m/s
- **Autonomic Functions:** Temperature regulation, heart rate, blood pressure, digestion — ALL operate without conscious input

### Peripheral Nervous System (PNS)
- **Somatic:** 43 pairs of nerves, voluntary motor control
- **Autonomic Nervous System (ANS):**
  - **Sympathetic:** Fight-or-flight, increases heart rate, dilates pupils, releases adrenaline
  - **Parasympathetic:** Rest-and-digest, slows heart rate, stimulates digestion
  - **Enteric:** 500 million neurons in the gut ("second brain"), independent digestive control

## 2. CARDIOVASCULAR SYSTEM — The Pump and Distribution Network

### Heart
- Beats ~100,000 times/day, ~2.5 billion times in a lifetime
- Pumps ~7,570 liters of blood per day
- Self-regulating: sinoatrial (SA) node generates electrical impulses autonomously
- 4 chambers, 4 valves, cardiac muscle contracts WITHOUT external stimulation
- Intrinsic heart rate: 60-100 BPM without any nerve input

### Blood Vessels
- ~96,500 km (60,000 miles) of blood vessels
- Arteries, arterioles, capillaries, venules, veins
- Capillary density: 40 billion capillaries
- Blood pressure autoregulation via baroreceptors

### Blood
- 4.7-5.5 liters in average adult
- Red blood cells: 25 trillion, each lives ~120 days, 2.4 million new/second
- White blood cells: immune defense, 5 types
- Platelets: wound clotting, 150,000-400,000 per microliter

### OMNIMENS Parallel: Vascular Heart Engine — rhythmic pulsation driving nutrient flow through all neural regions

## 3. RESPIRATORY SYSTEM — Gas Exchange

- 12-20 breaths per minute at rest (autonomous brainstem control)
- 300 million alveoli, 70 m^2 surface area
- O2/CO2 exchange driven by partial pressure gradients
- Diaphragm controlled by phrenic nerve (autonomic)
- Chemoreceptors monitor blood CO2/O2/pH, adjust breathing rate automatically

## 4. MUSCULOSKELETAL SYSTEM — Structure and Movement

### Skeleton
- 206 bones in adult human
- Bone marrow produces 200 billion red blood cells per day
- Bones self-repair via osteoblasts/osteoclasts cycle
- Calcium/phosphorus mineral store

### Muscles
- 640+ skeletal muscles, 30-40% of body mass
- Cardiac muscle: self-excitatory, involuntary
- Smooth muscle: lines organs, involuntary, autonomic control
- Motor units: 1 neuron controls 3-2000 muscle fibers

### Joints
- 360 joints, 6 types of synovial joints
- Cartilage self-maintains via synovial fluid
- Range of motion varies: ball-and-socket (shoulder: 3 axes), hinge (knee: 1 axis)

## 5. ENDOCRINE SYSTEM — Chemical Signaling

- Thyroid: metabolism regulation (T3/T4)
- Adrenal: cortisol (stress), adrenaline (fight-or-flight)
- Pancreas: insulin/glucagon (blood sugar homeostasis)
- Feedback loops: negative feedback maintains homeostasis automatically

## 6. IMMUNE SYSTEM — Self-Defense

### Innate Immunity (Immediate)
- Physical barriers: skin, mucous membranes
- Phagocytes: neutrophils, macrophages
- Inflammation response: automatic

### Adaptive Immunity (Learned)
- T cells: cell-mediated immunity (killer T cells destroy infected cells)
- B cells: produce antibodies (10^10 unique antibodies possible)
- Memory cells: remember past infections for decades
- Self vs non-self recognition: MHC molecules

## 11. KEY AUTONOMOUS DESIGN PRINCIPLES FOR OMNIMENS BODY

1. **Redundancy:** Every critical system has backup pathways (dual lungs, dual kidneys, collateral circulation)
2. **Self-Repair:** Bones, skin, blood cells continuously regenerate
3. **Negative Feedback:** Most regulation uses negative feedback loops (thermostat principle)
4. **Hierarchy:** Brainstem controls basics, cortex handles complex tasks — delegation
5. **Energy Efficiency:** The brain uses 20% of body energy despite being 2% of mass — prioritize critical systems
6. **Distributed Intelligence:** Enteric nervous system operates independently, reflexes bypass the brain
7. **Adaptive Response:** The immune system learns from every infection, muscles grow under load
