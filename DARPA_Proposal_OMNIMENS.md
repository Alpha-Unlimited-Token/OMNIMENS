## SECTION 3: TECHNICAL APPROACH

### 3.1 Problem Statement

Current autonomous AI systems suffer from critical limitations:

- **Dependency on external AI providers** — Most "autonomous" systems are wrappers around GPT, Claude, or Gemini. Remove the API key and they stop functioning entirely.
- **No genuine reasoning** — Current systems perform pattern matching, not causal or deductive reasoning.
- **No self-modification** — No deployed system can safely rewrite its own source code, test the changes, and restart with improvements.
- **No hardware adaptability** — AI code runs on cloud servers. It cannot compile to embedded processors for field deployment on robots, drones, or edge devices.
- **No emotional or motivational modeling** — Systems lack the drives that produce adaptive, context-sensitive behavior in biological intelligence.
