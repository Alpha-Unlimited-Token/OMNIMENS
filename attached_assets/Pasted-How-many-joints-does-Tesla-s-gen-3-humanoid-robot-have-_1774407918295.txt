Tesla's Gen 3 Humanoid Robot (Optimus) is designed with roughly 50 total actuators, featuring advanced, highly articulated hands that possess 22 degrees of freedom (DoF) to achieve human-level precision, notes BASENOR - Tesla Accessories. The hand updates include 11 DoF in fingers and 3 DoF in the wrist, enabling tasks like handling delicate objects and improved, natural movement, says BASENOR - Tesla Accessories. 
Key details regarding the Gen 3 / 2026 update:
Total Actuators: ~50 total actuators throughout the body, according to BASENOR - Tesla Accessories.
Hand Articulation: 22 Degrees of Freedom (DoF) per hand, notes Reddit.
Functionality: Designed for "superhuman" precision in tasks such as folding clothes, manipulating, and assembling, according to Not a Tesla App.
Capacity: The robot is designed to handle 45 lbs, according to Not a Tesla App.
